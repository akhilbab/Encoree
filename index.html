EENCORE WORK
Pilot launching in one French metropolitan area

Experience is not a retirement problem. It is a shortage solution.

Encore Work connects experienced people aged 60 to 75 with verified, flexible jobs matched to their skills and working preferences, helping them earn on their terms while employers fill short-term needs with proven talent.

How it works

1

Build a skills profile

Workers describe their experience, availability, location and preferred conditions. Profiles and references are shared only with consent.

2

Get matched

Employers post verified assignments with written pay, hours and job demands. Matching looks at skills, schedule and distance only.

3

Start with support

Assisted onboarding and human support for both sides. Workers can decline any offer, at any time.

Design principles we do not negotiate

  • Voluntary participation and freedom to decline.
  • No age based ranking, and regular audits of matching outcomes.
  • Clear job demands, pay and hours.
  • Consent based profiles and references.
  • Accessible onboarding and human support.
  • No assumption about reliability or capacity from age.

With an assumed average service base of €600 per assignment, 30 assignments represent €18,000 of base and about €900 in fees. The pilot aims to validate the model and local density, not to make a profit.

Five sales tactics

1. Direct SME outreach

Approach SMEs with recurring administrative or reception tasks, opening with the cost of a two week backlog.

Weeks 2 to 12 · KPI: meetings booked, first assignments

2. Local institutions

Partner with the chamber of commerce, business clubs and senior associations to reach both sides.

Weeks 1 to 8 · KPI: partners signed, leads per partner

3. Worker events and referrals

Hold information sessions in associations and community centres, and ask each placed worker for one referral.

Weeks 3 to 12 · KPI: verified profiles, referral rate

4. Staffing agency partnership

Offer agencies a shared pool of experienced candidates for short missions they cannot staff.

Weeks 4 to 10 · KPI: agency assignments, fee share

5. Pilot offer and proof

No fee on a first assignment, then short case stories published with consent.

Weeks 5 to 12 · KPI: conversion of offer to repeat order

Trust and FAQ

Questions we expect

Do you rank people by age?

No. Matching uses skills, schedule and distance only, and we audit outcomes to check that no age pattern appears in shortlists.

Is participation voluntary?

Yes. Workers choose their availability and can decline any offer without penalty.

What about pension rules?

Rules depend on each person’s situation. We provide human support and refer workers to official pension guidance rather than giving advice ourselves.

How are employers verified?

Employers go through checks before listing, and every assignment shows pay, hours and contract terms in writing.

What contract route is used?

The lawful route is confirmed with the employer for each assignment, with support during onboarding. This is one of the points the pilot will test with partners.

What does it cost?

Nothing for workers. Employers pay a 5% success fee on the contractually defined service base, and only for completed assignments.

How is personal data handled?

Profiles and references are consent based. Workers decide what is shared and can withdraw at any time.

Where does the pilot run?

In one French metropolitan area, starting with temporary administrative support and customer service assignments for SMEs.
